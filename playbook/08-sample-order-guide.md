# 08: Ordering Samples on Printify

**Goal:** get real samples in hand. Use them to choose the best US print provider, and to film your first ads.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Printify doesn't offer free samples.** You pay the normal base price plus shipping. (Printify Premium lowers base prices, but it's not worth subscribing just for samples.)
- **Recommended order:**
  - 2 blankets, from 2 different providers
  - 1 canvas
  - 1 ornament
- **Estimated total: about $100–130** including shipping, before tax. **Check the total on the checkout screen before you pay.** If it's far above that, stop and tell me.
- **Timing:** about 2–5 business days to produce plus 3–5 to ship. Ordered by Sep 29, they should arrive around Oct 6–9.

## Step 1: Make the design file (about 15 minutes)

1. **Use the original photo from your phone.** Not a screenshot, and not a copy that went through text or social media; those are compressed. Pick one with good light, the face toward the camera, and eyes sharp.
2. **Cut the pet out of the background:**
   - **iPhone (iOS 16+):** open the photo, press and hold the pet until it glows, then tap Share → Save Image.
   - **Android or computer:** use a free background remover such as Adobe Express's.
3. **Test the hard case.** A dark-furred pet is the toughest thing to print, because fur detail can turn into a black blob. If you have access to one, include it.

## Step 2: Connect Printify to your store (about 5 minutes, free)

1. In Shopify admin, go to **Apps**, search **Printify**, and click **Install**.
2. Create your Printify account **inside that flow**, using the same email. Creating a separate account on printify.com is the most common mistake.
3. In Printify, check **My stores** to confirm the Shopify store shows as connected.
4. Add a payment method: Printify → Account → Billing.

## Step 3: Create the sample products (about 20 minutes)

**Do not click "Publish."** Only click "Save product." Publishing would push them to your Shopify store. The real, customizable products get created later through the personalizer app.

1. Go to **Catalog** and set **Ship from / target market = United States**.
2. **Choose a specific provider** for each product. **Don't use "Printify Choice,"** because it can route orders to different providers, and you need to know exactly which one you're testing.
   - Compare providers on price, production time, and **Performance Score** (quality, speed, and sample/shipping record).
3. **The blanket (twice, with two different providers):**
   - Search "sherpa blanket" and pick the **50×60** size.
   - Choose the provider with the highest Performance Score, and a second provider that's the best value.
   - Click **Start designing**, upload your cut-out pet photo, and size it large in the center.
   - Add the pet's name as text.
   - Set a solid background color if the editor offers one: warm cream, or a bold color, to test how bright colors print on fleece.
   - Watch the resolution warning. Blankets need about **120–150 DPI**. If it warns you, make the photo smaller on the blanket.
   - Click **Save product**.
4. **The canvas:**
   - Search "canvas" and pick **12×16** (or the closest size offered).
   - Use the same design, then save.
5. **The ornament:**
   - Search "ceramic ornament" and pick a circle or heart shape.
   - Use the same design, then save.
6. **Save money on shipping:** if one of your blanket providers also makes the canvas or ornament, use that provider. Extra items in the same shipment cost less to ship.

## Step 4: Place the sample order (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **Orders** (or My products), click **Create order**, then choose **Sample**.
2. Add the 4 sample products. Your account address fills in as the shipping address; check that it's right.
3. Pick standard shipping. Express only makes sense if you're badly behind schedule.
4. **Review the total** and submit. You'll get tracking emails from Printify.

## Step 5: When the samples arrive

**Film the unboxing before you open anything.** It's your first ad footage. Film:
- the box opening
- the blanket unfolding
- a reaction
- your pet lying on it
- texture close-ups

**Decide:**
- Use the blanket provider with the higher total, unless it's more than 2 days slower.
- If the landed cost you see on the checkout screen is **over $45** per blanket, tell me. We'll reprice to $89.95 or make the canvas the main product.
