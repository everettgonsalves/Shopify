# Shopify Launch Playbook: Q4 2026

Written Monday, September 28, 2026. Aimed at a brand-new store selling to US customers.

## The short version

**Product to launch:** a **custom pet portrait blanket**. The customer uploads a photo of their pet, the store turns it into artwork with the pet's name, and a US print-on-demand partner prints and ships it. It gets sold as a gift. Two add-ons sit next to it: a **canvas print** (better margin) and a **matching ornament** (raises order value). A **pet memorial** line keeps it selling after Christmas.

**Why this product, why now:**

1. **Timing.** Pet portraits and photo blankets sell most in Q4, as Christmas gifts and memorial keepsakes. Black Friday is Nov 27, 2026, 60 days from today.
2. **Ad costs.** Pet products have some of the best Meta ad numbers of any category in 2026: roughly a $22–38 cost per purchase and a 2.5–4.8x return on ad spend.
3. **Tariffs.** The US duty-free allowance for small packages ("de minimis") is still suspended. Every package from China now owes duties, which hurts classic dropshipping. Printing in the US avoids most of that.
4. **No inventory.** You don't pay for stock up front. Personalized items are usually non-returnable, and you replace defective ones with a reprint.
5. **Life after Q4.** Pet memorials, birthdays, Valentine's Day, and "dog mom" Mother's Day keep it selling after Christmas.

**The honest part:**

- **No product is a "$1k/day product."** $1k a day comes from product × offer × creative × ad spend × execution. Stores selling this category at scale already exist (Crown & Paw, West & Willow, and others). You won't win because the product is unique. You win on creative, offer, speed, and proof.
- **$1k a day in revenue is not $1k a day in profit.** With the numbers in [02-unit-economics.md](02-unit-economics.md), $1k a day earns about **$150/day profit at a $25 cost per purchase and about $0 at $37**. Your cost per purchase and your email revenue decide whether this works.
- **Most new stores never get close.** The often-quoted "90% of stores fail" figure can't be traced to any study. General US small-business data shows about 20% close in year one. Either way, plan on testing, not on a sure thing.

## The 10-week calendar (starting today)

| Dates | Phase | Must be done by the end |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Sep 28 – Oct 4** | Validate and build | 60-minute validation done. Samples ordered from 2 print providers. Store, apps, pages, and tracking live in password mode. TikTok account posting daily. |
| **Oct 5 – Oct 11** | Content and soft launch | Samples arrive. 10–15 ad creatives filmed. Test order placed. Store opens ~Oct 8. Meta test starts ~Oct 9 at $50–100/day. |
| **Oct 12 – Oct 25** | Test | 5–10 new creatives a week. Kill and scale rules applied daily. Email flows on. First real reviews coming in. Google Shopping started. |
| **Oct 26 – Nov 8** | Scale before ad prices spike | Winning ads scaled 20–30% every 48–72h while cost per purchase holds. Black Friday early-access list growing. |
| **Nov 9 – Nov 22** | Pre-peak | Christmas delivery deadline banner (use the real dates from your print provider). Black Friday creatives and offer ready. |
| **Nov 23 – Nov 30** | Black Friday / Cyber Monday | Offer live Thu Nov 26 to Mon Nov 30. Heavy email and SMS. Ad spend only on proven winners. |
| **Dec 1 – ~Dec 12** | Christmas push | "Order by X for Christmas" countdown using the **real** provider cutoff (last year's was Dec 12–16). |
| **~Dec 13 – Dec 31** | After the cutoff | Sell printable "gift reveal" cards and gift cards. Shift ads to the memorial angle. Lower spend. |
| **January** | Decide | Review the numbers against the kill and scale criteria. Build for Valentine's Day, memorials, and birthdays, or pivot. |
