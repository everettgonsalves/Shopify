# 04: Marketing and Launch

**Main idea:** under Meta's 2026 algorithm (Andromeda), **creative is the targeting.** Your job is to produce a high volume of genuinely different ads, let broad targeting find buyers, and cut losers fast. Organic TikTok and email are how you get to profit, because those sales don't cost ad money.

---

## 1. Creative strategy

### The 8 angles (each one is a separate concept, not a variation)

| # | Angle | Who it's for | Core emotion |
|---|-------|--------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Gift reveal reaction.** Someone unwraps the blanket and sees their pet's face. | Gift buyers | Joy, happy tears |
| 2 | **Pet reacts to itself.** The dog sniffs or lies on its own face. | Pet owners, for themselves | Funny, cute |
| 3 | **Before and after.** Blurry phone photo → gorgeous portrait blanket. | Skeptics ("will mine look good?") | Surprise, trust |
| 4 | **"The gift for the person who has everything."** Grandma, dad, the dog-mom friend. | Stuck gift shoppers | Relief |
| 5 | **Memorial.** "I get to hug him again." Gentle, no hard sell. | Grieving owners, their friends and family | Love, comfort |
| 6 | **Texture and ASMR.** Sherpa close-ups, the squish, a person curling up in it. | Comfort buyers | Coziness |
| 7 | **Process.** Order → preview → print → unbox, as a founder-style video. | Trust seekers | Transparency |
| 8 | **Deadline / offer** (November–December only). "Order by Dec X for Christmas," Black Friday bundle. | Warm audiences, late shoppers | Urgency (real) |

### 15 hooks for the first 2 seconds

1. "My mom cried for 10 minutes when she opened this."
2. "POV: your dog sees his own face on a blanket."
3. "I turned this blurry photo into THIS."
4. "The only gift my dad has ever actually used."
5. "If your dog is your whole personality, you need this."
6. "She passed in March. This Christmas I can still hug her."
7. "Stop buying your sister candles."
8. "Rating custom pet gifts until I find one that doesn't look creepy."
9. "My cat has never sat on anything I bought her. Except this."
10. "Every dog mom I know is getting this for Christmas."
11. "Here's how your blanket gets made."
12. "What $79 gets you vs. a $30 Amazon photo blanket."
13. "Grandparents of dogs, this one's for you."
14. "Last day to order for Christmas is [real date]."
15. "The gift that made my husband go quiet."

**Only use a claim if it's true for you.** Get real reactions from friends and family filming a real unboxing, with their permission, or from paid creators. Don't script "I cried" for someone who didn't.

### Formats per concept

- 9:16 video, 15–30 seconds (Reels, TikTok, Stories). **This is your main format.**
- 4:5 video (Feed).
- 1:1 or 4:5 static image: before/after, review screenshot, or "gift guide" graphic.
- 4:5 carousel: style options, or recipient types (Mom, Dad, Grandma, Memorial).

### Getting creatives made

- **Week 1: your own footage.** Use your samples: 2 blankets, 1 canvas, ornaments. Film:
  - your pet or friends' pets on the blanket
  - hands and texture close-ups
  - an unboxing
  - a real reaction from a family member you surprise
- **Creators:** 3–5 pet micro-creators (5k–50k followers). Send each a free blanket of *their* pet in exchange for content plus usage rights. Find them through:
  - Instagram/TikTok searches (#dogmom plus your city)
  - TikTok Creator Marketplace
  - paid platforms like Billo or Insense (about $100–200 per video)
- **Quota:** **10–15 creatives at launch, then 5–10 new ones every week.** Andromeda rewards 10–30 distinct creatives per ad set. Five versions of the same video with different text don't count as different.

## 2. Meta ads setup (main paid channel)

### Account setup
- Business Manager with the domain verified.
- Pixel and Conversions API through the Shopify Meta app.
- Payment card on file with a high limit.
- Instagram account connected.

### Structure at small budgets ($50–150/day)

Advantage+ Shopping needs about 50 purchases a week to learn well, which you won't have yet. Use this instead:

```
Campaign: Sales – Prospecting – Broad    (Campaign budget, $50–100/day to start)
  └─ Ad set: US, 25–65+, all genders, no interests (broad), Advantage+ placements
       Optimization: Purchase     Attribution: 7-day click, 1-day view
       └─ 10–15 ads = 10–15 distinct concepts (see angles above)
```

- Add a second ad set only to test a truly different variable, like a canvas-only landing page. Don't split by interests.
- **Retargeting:** no separate campaign until you're spending about $150+/day. Broad campaigns already retarget warm audiences. In November, add a small retargeting campaign ($15–30/day) for "added to cart / started checkout" in the last 14 days, running offer and deadline creatives.

### Kill and scale rules (check once a day, same time)

Target cost per purchase = **$32 or lower**. Break-even is about $37 (see file 02).

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| An ad spent **$60+** (about 1.5–2x target) with **0 purchases** and a weak click rate (below ~0.8%) | Turn it off |
| An ad spent **$60+** with 0 purchases but a strong click rate and add-to-carts | Give it one more $30–40, then decide |
| Campaign cost per purchase **≤ $32** for 3 straight days | Raise budget **20–30%** every 48–72h |
| Campaign cost per purchase **$32–40** | Hold. Add new creatives. Work on the product page and order value. |
| Campaign cost per purchase **above $45** for 3+ days after the first week | Cut budget 30%, refresh creatives, and check the product page and checkout for problems |
| A winning ad's frequency above ~3 and cost per purchase rising | Add new variations of **that concept** (new hook, new creator) |

**Don't** edit ads or ad sets more than once a day, and **don't** judge anything on less than 24–48h of data. Every edit resets learning.

### Black Friday and Q4 on Meta
- CPMs rise 20–50% across Q4 and 50–80%+ in Black Friday week. **Your cheapest learning happens now (October).**
- During Black Friday week, only spend on proven winners. **Don't test new ideas at peak prices.**
- Budget changes take hours to kick in. Raise Black Friday budgets **Wed Nov 25**, not Friday morning.

## 3. TikTok (organic first, then ads)

- **Organic, starting this week:** 1–2 posts a day from the brand account. Film the creation process, pet reactions, the "blurry photo → portrait" series, and customer unboxings (with permission). Organic TikTok costs nothing and doubles as testing: **your best organic video is your next ad.**
- **Spark Ads** (boosting organic posts) once a post beats your usual views by 3–5x.
- **TikTok Shop:** optional later. Personalization flows are clunkier there. Get Shopify working first.

## 4. Google Shopping (intent capture from week 3)

- Set up Google Merchant Center through the Google & YouTube app.
- Product titles like "Custom Pet Portrait Blanket – Personalized Dog & Cat Photo Sherpa Blanket."
- Start with a **Standard Shopping** campaign at $15–25/day (exact search terms like "custom dog blanket" and "personalized pet blanket").
- Performance Max comes later, once you have conversion data. People searching these terms in December are buyers who are ready to purchase.

## 5. Email and SMS (Klaviyo): where the profit comes from

**Signup popup:**
- Offer: "10% off your first custom gift + our Christmas deadline reminder."
- Show it after 8 seconds or on exit intent, on mobile too.

**Flows to build before launch.** Copy is in file 06.

| Flow | Trigger | Messages | Benchmark |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Welcome** | Popup signup | 3 emails over 5 days: code → how it works and social proof → gift ideas and deadline | About $2.65 revenue per recipient on average |
| **Abandoned checkout** | Started checkout, no order | 3 emails (1h, 20h, 48h) + 1 SMS if opted in | About $3.65 revenue per recipient; top 10% reach about $29 |
| **Abandoned cart** | Added to cart | 2 emails | – |
| **Browse abandonment** | Viewed product (known email) | 1–2 emails | – |
| **Post-purchase** | Order placed | Thank you and what happens next → shipped → review request 5 days after delivery (**ask permission to use their pet photo in ads**) → "Who else is on your list?" second-gift offer | Repeat purchases |

**Campaigns (sends to your whole list):**
- 1–2 a week in October.
- 3–4 a week from Nov 16 to the Christmas cutoff: early access, Black Friday live, Cyber Monday, deadline reminders.

## 6. Offers by phase

| Phase | Offer | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Launch (October) | Free shipping + 10% welcome code + reprint guarantee | Low friction without training people to wait for discounts |
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Nov 26 – Nov 30) | **Blanket + ornament bundle $89.95** (you save $9.95) **or** 20% off sitewide. Pick one and test it on your list first. | A bundle protects margin better than a deep discount |
| December to cutoff | "Order by [real date] for Christmas delivery" + bundle | Real urgency |
| After cutoff | "Gift it today": instant **printable gift reveal card** with the blanket arriving in January + digital gift cards | Keeps sales going after shipping cutoffs |
| January+ | Memorial, birthdays, Valentine's ("Galentine's for dog moms") | Year-round demand |

## 7. Week-by-week launch calendar

| Week | Marketing tasks |
|------|-----------------|
| **Sep 28 – Oct 4** | Accounts set up (Meta Business Manager, TikTok, Klaviyo). Order samples. Start posting on TikTok daily (process videos, pet content). Contact 10 micro-creators. Build flows. |
| **Oct 5 – Oct 11** | Film 10–15 concepts from samples. Launch the Meta Broad campaign at $50–100/day (~Oct 9). Popup live. |
| **Oct 12 – Oct 18** | Daily kill/scale checks. 5–10 new creatives. Creator videos arrive. First organic winners → Spark Ads test. |
| **Oct 19 – Oct 25** | Launch Google Shopping. Canvas-only test if blankets are weak. Checkpoint: **$1,000–1,200 spent, with a clear yes or no on the kill rule (file 01).** |
| **Oct 26 – Nov 8** | Scale winners 20–30% every 2–3 days. Launch the Black Friday early-access signup ("Get first access + free ornament"). Memorial page live. |
| **Nov 9 – Nov 22** | Publish the provider's holiday deadlines on the site. Deadline creatives ready. Black Friday creatives and emails scheduled. |
| **Nov 23 – Nov 30** | Black Friday: early access Wed/Thu to the email list, live Fri–Mon. Raise ad budgets on Wed. Keep only winners on. |
| **Dec 1 – ~Dec 12** | "Last day for Christmas" countdown (email, SMS, ads, site banner). Retargeting at full strength. |
| **~Dec 13 – Dec 31** | Printable gift reveal cards and gift cards. Lower spend. Memorial and "New Year" angles. |
| **January** | Full review against file 05 numbers. Decide whether to scale into Valentine's Day, memorials, and birthdays, or pivot. |
