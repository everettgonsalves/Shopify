Takeaways:

- **The canvas makes more per sale.** The blanket wins on gift appeal and on video, because hugging it, the texture, and the pet lying on it all film well.
- If your real blanket landed cost is above $45, go to $89.95 or make the canvas the hero product.

## 2. Blended order

Planning assumptions:

- 70% of orders lead with a blanket and 30% with a canvas.
- 25% of orders add an ornament.
- 3% of revenue is set aside for reprints and refunds.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|------:|
| **Average order value** | **$80.44** |
| Profit per order before ads | $39.65 |
| Profit per order after the 3% reserve | **$37.24 (46%)** |
| **Break-even cost per purchase** | **~$37** |
| **Break-even return on ad spend** | **~2.16x** |

**Meaning:** any ad that gets purchases for less than about $37 each makes money, before fixed costs.

Pet-category benchmarks run $22–38 per purchase. **You're competitive, but not by much.** In Q4, CPMs rise 20–80%, so you need better-than-average creative, a higher order value, or email revenue to have real room.

## 3. What $1,000/day actually requires

- **Orders:** $1,000 ÷ $80.44 = **about 12.4 orders a day**.
- **Store visits:** about 620 a day at 2% conversion, or about 830 a day at 1.5%.

Two columns below:

- **"All paid"** assumes every order came from ads.
- **"25% organic/email"** assumes a quarter of orders came from email, SMS, TikTok organic, or direct traffic.

| Cost per purchase | Ad spend/day (all paid) | Profit/day (all paid) | Ad spend/day (25% organic/email) | Profit/day (25% organic/email) | Profit/month (25% organic/email) |
|------------------:|------------------------:|----------------------:|---------------------------------:|-------------------------------:|---------------------------------:|
| $25 | $311 | **$152** | $233 | **$230** | ~$6,900 |
| $32 | $398 | $65 | $298 | $165 | ~$4,900 |
| $37 | $460 | $3 | $345 | $118 | ~$3,500 |
| $45 | $559 | **–$96** | $420 | $43 | ~$1,300 |

Subtract fixed costs of about **$200–300 a month**: Shopify Basic (~$39), personalizer (~$19–49), Klaviyo, and a reviews app.

**The two things that decide profit:**

1. **Cost per purchase.** Creative quality and volume drive this. See file 04.
2. **Share of revenue that doesn't come from ads.** Email and SMS flows, TikTok organic, and repeat buyers (people ordering a second gift). **Aim for 20–30% by December.**

**Track MER (blended return on ad spend) daily:** total store revenue ÷ total ad spend. Break-even MER is about **2.2**. Target **3.0+**.

## 4. Budget tiers

| Tier | Total cash | How it splits | What you can expect |
|------|-----------:|---------------|---------------------|
| **Minimum** | ~$1,500 | $150 samples, $150 apps and plan, **$1,000–1,200 ads** at $50–60/day for about 3 weeks | Enough to find out whether a winning ad exists. Not enough to scale into Black Friday. |
| **Recommended** | $3,000–5,000 | $150 samples, $200 apps, $300–600 creator videos, **$2,000–3,500 ads**, **$500+ cash buffer** | Test in October and scale into November if the numbers hold. $1k/day becomes possible, not certain. |
| **Aggressive** | $8,000+ | Same, with bigger creator budgets and $200–400/day by November | Only after the $1,000–1,200 test proves cost per purchase is below $37. |

**Never put in money you can't lose.** Q4 is the best window for this product, and it's also the most expensive time to advertise.

## 5. Cash-flow traps

1. **Print providers charge you when the order is placed.** Shopify Payments pays you out on a cycle, typically a few business days. Printify and Printful need a funded balance or card. At 12 orders a day you'll pay about $450 a day in print costs before payouts land.
2. **Meta bills your card** at spending thresholds, often several times a day once you scale. Use a card with a high limit.
3. **Payout holds and reserves.** New stores with fast growth can get a **reserve of up to 20% held for up to 120 days**, or a payout pause. Your best defense:
   - fast, trackable fulfillment
   - clear "arrives by" dates
   - quick support replies
   - verified business details in Shopify

   **Keep at least 2 weeks of ad spend plus print costs as a buffer.**
4. **Chargebacks.** Keep them well below 1% (Shopify's risk flag) and below Visa's 1.5% threshold (North America, since March 2026).
   - Most chargebacks come from late or unclear deliveries and unrecognizable card-statement names.
   - Set your statement descriptor to your brand name.

## 6. Break-even formulas (for your own numbers)

```
Profit per order before ads  = AOV − landed cost − payment fees − app fees − reprint/refund reserve
Break-even cost per purchase = Profit per order before ads
Break-even ROAS              = AOV ÷ Profit per order before ads
Orders needed per day        = Revenue target ÷ AOV
Daily profit                 = Orders × Profit per order − Ad spend − (Monthly fixed costs ÷ 30)
```
