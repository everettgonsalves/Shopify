# 01: Product Research

## 1. First, a challenge to the question

"Find me a $1k/day product" assumes the product is what produces the $1k. It mostly isn't. The same product earns $0 for one store and $5k/day for another. The difference is:

- **Offer:** price, bundle, guarantee, and gift framing.
- **Creative:** whether the ad stops the scroll and makes someone want the thing.
- **Unit economics:** whether you can afford to pay $30–40 to acquire a customer.
- **Execution speed:** how many creatives you test per week and how fast you cut losers.

So the goal here is narrower and more honest: **pick the product with the highest odds of reaching $1k/day in Q4 2026 for a brand-new store, with limited money and no audience, while losing the least if it fails.**

## 2. The market in September 2026: the constraints that shaped the pick

| Factor | What's true now | What it means for you |
|--------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **US customs (de minimis)** | The $800 duty-free allowance for small packages was suspended for China in May 2025 and for all countries on Aug 29, 2025. When the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs (Feb 20, 2026), the suspension was **kept** by a separate executive order. Postal flat duties of $80–200 per item have applied to some routes. | Shipping single orders from China is expensive and slow at customs. **Fulfill from the US**, either US print-on-demand or US-warehoused stock. |
| **China tariffs** | The IEEPA tariffs are gone. Section 301 China tariffs remain, and new Section 301 "forced-labor" tariffs of 10–12.5% on 60 economies replaced the 10% Section 122 tariff on Jul 24, 2026. | Anything made in China and imported by you carries meaningful duty. Get landed, duty-paid (DDP) quotes, never the factory price alone. |
| **Meta ad costs** | Median e-commerce CPM (cost per 1,000 ad views) is about $13–17 in the US. Meta CPMs rose about 20% year over year in 2025. Q4 CPMs rise 20–50%, and 50–80%+ in Black Friday week. | Launch **now** so your ads learn in October while CPMs are lower. Don't make Black Friday week your first test. |
| **Meta's algorithm (Andromeda)** | Broad targeting with many *genuinely different* creatives beats detailed interest targeting. Advantage+ Shopping needs about 50 purchases a week to learn well. | Creative volume is your main lever. At small budgets, use a manual sales campaign with broad targeting (details in file 04). |
| **Conversion benchmarks** | Median Shopify conversion is about 1.4–2.1%. 3% puts you in the top 20%. Mobile averages about 1.2%. | Plan on 1.5–2% conversion. Build mobile-first. |
| **Pet category ad benchmarks** | Pet products on Meta average about a $25 cost per purchase (range $22–38) and a 2.5–4.8x return on ad spend. That's among the best of any vertical. | Pet emotion stops the scroll on its own. That's a structural advantage. |
| **Q4 seasonality** | Q4 is 40–60% of annual revenue for print-on-demand and gift sellers. Pet portraits and photo blankets peak in Q4, both as Christmas gifts and as memorial keepsakes. | Launch the gift angle now and let the memorial angle carry you after Christmas. |
| **Payout risk** | Shopify can hold payouts or keep a reserve (up to 20% for up to 120 days) on new stores with sudden volume. Visa's chargeback threshold tightened to 1.5% in North America in March 2026. | Keep a cash buffer. Fast, trackable fulfillment and clear delivery promises protect your payouts. |

## 3. The candidates and where they came from

Everything that appeared in several fall 2026 trend sources and fits a new store:

1. **Personalized pet products** (portrait blankets, canvases, ornaments, memorial pieces). Source: Printify/Printway Q4 guidance and personalized-gift market reports showing about 68% of online shoppers interested in custom goods.
2. **Red-light LED face masks.** Source: repeated "hot product" lists and at-home beauty-device growth. The mask market is growing about 9–12% a year.
3. **Heated apparel and heated wraps.** Source: fall 2026 lists. Heated apparel is projected to grow 20–25% a year, and searches peak in late Nov and December.
4. **Sleep products** (sunrise and sleep-trainer clocks, sleep masks). Source: the "sleepmaxxing" trend. About 45% of Americans say they do some form of it.
5. Considered and dropped early:
   - **Supplements.** Regulated claims, needs brand trust, high cost per purchase.
   - **LED projectors and cheap gadgets.** Around $15 retail can't absorb a $25+ cost per purchase on Meta; that model only works through TikTok Shop affiliates.
   - **Mouth tape.** A 2026 systematic review found little scientific support, and there are safety concerns.

## 5. The pick in detail: custom pet portrait blanket

### What you sell

- **Hero product:** Custom Pet Portrait Sherpa Blanket, 50×60 in. The customer uploads a photo, picks a style and background, and adds the pet's name. **$79.95, free shipping.**
- **Second product:** the same artwork on canvas, 12×16 in. **$64.95.** This has the better margin and becomes your main product if blanket ads underperform.
- **Checkout add-on:** a matching ceramic ornament with the same artwork. **$19.95.**
- **Bundle:** blanket + ornament for **$89.95.** This is also your Black Friday anchor.
- **Memorial line (live by mid-November):** the same products with memorial wording and a gentler page. Pet loss is year-round demand. The pet-memorial market is estimated at about $486M in 2026 and growing about 9% a year.

### Evidence of demand

- The personalized-gift market is about $31–35B in 2025–26, growing about 9% a year, with about 74% of sales online.
- The custom pet products market is about $8.5B (2025), growing about 8.4% a year. Pet portraits are the leading subcategory, and blankets and pillows are strong seasonal sellers.
- Top Shopify pet-portrait stores (Crown & Paw, West & Willow, Social Print Studio) each rank for 5,000+ organic keywords with six-figure monthly traffic value. Proven demand, and also proof that the market is contested.
- Custom pet blankets get consistent organic traction on TikTok (#dogmom, #petparentsoftiktok, gift-reveal reactions).
- On Meta, pet has the best benchmark return on ad spend of any vertical in 2026 datasets (about 4.8x average).

### Competitors and where you can beat them

| Competitor type | Their strength | Where you can win |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Crown & Paw, Purr & Mutt ("pet in a royal costume" style) | Brand, SEO, reviews | **Style.** Costume portraits are the look of 2019–2022. In 2026 demand is shifting to **minimalist, line-art, watercolor, and pop-art** styles. Lead with a modern style. |
| West & Willow (minimalist) | Clean look, decor positioning | **The blanket and the gift moment.** Lead with the huggable blanket and the reaction video, not wall decor. |
| Etsy sellers ($40–70) | Low price, marketplace traffic | **Speed and certainty.** An instant preview, a clear "arrives by" date, and a reprint guarantee. Etsy often means waiting for a proof and unclear timing. |
| Amazon photo blankets ($25–45) | Price, Prime | **Artwork plus emotion.** A plain photo blanket is a commodity. A styled portrait with the pet's name is a keepsake. |

### Risks, and what you do about each

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Crowded category keeps cost per purchase above $37 (your break-even; see file 02) | Test the canvas as a second product. Push the ornament add-on and bundle to raise order value. Build email revenue so 20%+ of sales don't cost ad money. Set a kill date (below). |
| Print quality varies by provider | Order samples from **2 providers** this week. Pick by the physical sample, not the listing. |
| Customer uploads a blurry photo, then gets a bad result and a reprint | Upload guidance with good and bad examples. Manually check every photo in the first 100 orders. Minimum resolution check in the personalizer app. |
| AI art styles copying Disney, Ghibli, or other named IP | **Don't offer them**, even though some personalizer apps do. Use generic style names like "Watercolor," "Line Art," or "Pop Art." |
| Christmas delivery cutoffs (Dec 12–16 last year for priority blankets) | Show real cutoff dates on the site. After the cutoff, switch to printable gift-reveal cards. |
| Business collapses after Q4 | Memorial line, birthdays, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day ("dog mom"), and new-puppy gifts. |

### Kill criteria

Decide these now, before emotions get involved.

- After **$1,000–1,200 of ad spend** (about 2–3 weeks): if **no single ad set** has reached a cost per purchase under $45 with at least 3 purchases, stop scaling blankets. Move spend to the canvas for one more $500 test.
- If after **$2,000 total** the blended cost per purchase is still above $45 (break-even is about $37), pause paid ads. Keep only organic and email running, and reassess the product.

## 6. Runners-up: why not now

- **LED face mask.** Good order value ($99–149) and gift fit. But:
  - Most face-mask claims (wrinkles, acne) make it a Class II medical device needing FDA 510(k) clearance, and the FTC requires substantiation.
  - Private-label stock costs $28–45 per unit with a 50-unit minimum, before duty.
  - Electronics bring "it doesn't work" returns.
  - Strong incumbents compete at every price.
  - **Better as a planned 2027 brand** with a cleared supplier, not a 60-day Q4 test.
- **Heated vest or neck wrap.** Great in Q4, then gone by February. Lithium batteries complicate shipping, vests bring sizing returns, and cheap Amazon options set the price.
- **Kids' sleep-trainer clock.** Solid product, but you're between Hatch ($145–170) and dozens of $40–60 Amazon clones, with China-made electronics carrying duty.

## 7. The 60-minute validation checklist

Do this before spending anything on ads. I couldn't reach these tools live, so you're the one who can close this gap.

1. **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library, country US, all ads). Search `custom pet blanket`, `dog portrait blanket`, and `pet memorial blanket`.
   - Count advertisers with ads **running 30+ days**. Long-running ads are profitable ads.
   - Screenshot the 10 longest-running ones. Their hooks, formats, and offers are your creative brief.
   - ✅ Proceed if you see 5+ advertisers with long-running ads (demand is proven and ads are profitable).
   - ⚠️ Rethink if you see nothing at all (there may be no paid demand) or if 3–4 brands dominate every result.
2. **Google Trends** (US, past 5 years). Compare `custom dog blanket`, `pet portrait`, and `custom pet portrait`. Confirm the Q4 spike, and check that the Q4 2025 peak is at least as high as Q4 2024.
3. **TikTok Creative Center** (Top Ads, US, Pets or Home category). Look for pet gift ads with high 6-second view rates. Save hooks.
4. **Etsy.** Search `custom pet blanket`. Note the number of results, the price range, and which listings show "Bestseller" or "In X baskets." This is your price and quality benchmark.
5. **Print provider quotes.** In Printify (and Printful or CustomCat), price the **50×60 sherpa blanket, 12×16 canvas, and ceramic ornament** from a **US print provider**, including shipping to a US address. Put the real numbers into the model in file 02. If blanket landed cost is above $45, raise the price to $89.95 or lead with the canvas.
6. **Order samples** of the blanket, canvas, and ornament from 2 providers, using your own pet photo (or a friend's) in 2 styles. These samples become your first ad footage.
